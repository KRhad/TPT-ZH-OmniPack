v1.0.0-rc1 - OmniPack pure-sandbox content freeze
- Removed player tasks, achievements, challenges, technology trees, alchemy progression, and forced unlocks
- Added a directly selectable 118-element periodic table with stable save mappings
- Frozen the catalog at 487 active non-alias types, 484 creatable types, and 466 directly selectable menu materials
- Added inorganic chemistry, engineering alloys, minerals, ceramics, glass, isotopes, organics, polymers, electronics, ecology, and pollution systems
- Kept stable identifiers, 10-bit element capacity, compatibility aliases, module-disable behavior, and OPS roundtrips
- Standardized OmniPack menu codes as unique four-letter uppercase labels and rewrote player descriptions without development notes
- Aligned the periodic-table and element-search shortcuts in the right toolbar and retained the normal element-row width
- Added official-style soft gas rendering for periodic, inorganic, isotope, and organic gases
- Repaired negative or stale element counts after dense mixed reactions with a cheap invariant check and on-demand exact recount
- Added deterministic Windows release-candidate, detached-symbol, license-manifest, PE, path, ZIP, and source-archive audits
- This local candidate is unsigned and remains release_ready=false until all stress, long-run, GUI/DPI, public-source, tag, and release gates pass
